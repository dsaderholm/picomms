My headset won't work

  1. Is the headset switched on and charged? Switch it off and on again.
  2. Stand closer to the intercom box (or the crew area) and wait 10 seconds. The headset should connect by itself.
  3. Still nothing? Tell the crew lead. They can press Reconnect in the admin page. Never pair the headset to your own phone or Chromebook — it will stop connecting here.

Mute, groups and all-call

  • Mute stops other people hearing you. You can still hear everything.
  • Talk to chooses which group hears you. Listen chooses which groups you hear.
  • All-call (only some people) lets everyone hear you, whatever group they are in.
  • Call Sound puts a flashing alert on the sound operator's screen. It works even if you are muted and whatever the captions heard.
  • Show mode locks settings (pairing, names, groups). It never stops anyone hearing or talking.

Whispering

There is no voice gate on the intercom. Whispers and the start of sentences are not cut off. If your microphone seems too quiet, move the boom closer to the corner of your mouth rather than speaking louder.

Captions

Captions are automatic and can be wrong or late. They are an aid, not a record. They are only kept if an administrator has explicitly turned that on. If captions stop, the intercom audio is not affected.
