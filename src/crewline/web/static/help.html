Quick answers

My headset won't connect

  1. Switch it off and on again. Make sure it is charged.
  2. Stand near the crew area and wait ten seconds. It should connect by itself.
  3. Still nothing? Tell the crew lead, who can press Reconnect in Admin.
  4. Never pair the headset with your own phone or Chromebook. It will stop connecting here until it is forgotten there.

Mute, groups and all-call

  • Mute stops other people hearing you. You still hear everything.
  • Talk to picks who hears you. Listen picks which groups you hear.
  • All-call (only some people) lets everyone hear you for a moment. Muting or choosing a group ends it.
  • Call Sound flashes an alert on the sound operator's screen. It works while muted and does not depend on captions.
  • Show mode locks settings such as pairing, names and groups. It never stops anyone hearing or talking.

When my name is mentioned

If someone says your name, the captions page flashes violet and, if you chose it, your earpiece plays two soft pings. It is a hint, not a promise: captions can miss a name. If it is urgent, someone should call you directly.

Whispering

There is no voice gate on the intercom, so whispers and the start of a sentence are not cut off. If you sound too quiet, move the boom closer to the corner of your mouth instead of speaking louder.

Too bright in the booth?

Use the theme button at the top of any page. Night uses dim red only, which keeps your eyes ready for the dark.

Captions

Captions are automatic and can be wrong or late. They are an aid, not a record. Nothing is stored unless an administrator has turned that on. If captions stop, the intercom audio is not affected.
